# T-Zero identity

The orbital monogram combines a T, a zero-shaped orbit, and a navigation star. Mint and icy white sit against the application's near-black navy background.

`tzero-logo-master.png` is the selected image generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. `tzero-logo.png`, `tzero-icon.png`, and `apple-touch-icon.png` are size-specific exports. The root `logo.png`, favicon, Android source logo, and splash use this same identity. Recreate renditions with `node scripts/generate-brand-assets.cjs`.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Produce the FINAL production T-Zero app logo on a completely solid uniform near-black navy background #080C12. Preserve the orbital T/zero monogram, sweeping diagonal ring and four-point star of the reference. Make the mark slightly smaller with generous 18% clear padding on all sides. Use exceptionally crisp smooth graphic curves and clean edges, entirely solid mint #8CE6DA for the monogram and icy white #EAF0F5 for the orbit/star. Flat professional graphic design, simple high-impact silhouette. Crucially all space around and inside the symbol must be pure uniform dark navy, with NO speckles, NO distressed texture, NO edge noise, NO grain, NO shadows, NO bevels, NO photography, NO gradients, NO letters or words. A polished square app identity. Opaque background, no transparency.
